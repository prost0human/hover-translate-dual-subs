# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive and welcoming. Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Assume good faith, focus feedback on the work, and respect different languages, experience levels and access needs.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions or restrict participation to protect the community. For a public report use this fork's GitHub issue tracker; do not include private information or credentials in a public issue. Sensitive reports should be sent through a private contact channel configured by the repository owner. The fork's maintainers, not the upstream project, are responsible for responding to its community issues.
